## Text pass

- Read every label aloud. The wording sounds natural, uses clear actions, and keeps the facts. Replace shorthand such as "Clean + size sort" with "Wash and sort". Check for banned punctuation and decorative separators.
- Capitalization follows the skill's capitalization rule: sentence case for prose labels, uppercase for structural headings, and exact source spelling for identifiers. Check branch labels and legends too. wrapped lines continue the original sentence.
- Every heading, body line, subtitle, annotation, and footer is fully visible with breathing room. Check the longest line in each column first.
- Padding is consistent. short boxes are not made to fit by shrinking text. Markers share a clean indent with both heading and subtitle.
- Decision text sits within the sloping sides with visible padding above and below.
- Section tags have a separate row when required. Long notes and legends wrap cleanly.
- At reading width, secondary text remains readable and the hierarchy is clear.

## Connector pass

- Each start/end is attached to the intended node or section. Arrowheads touch its boundary and point into it.
- The first and final segments follow the selected port axis. A top/bottom port uses a vertical segment. a left/right port uses a horizontal segment. Turns happen after a clear stub, never at the vertex itself.
- Each decision has an incoming path and named outcomes. The yes/no labels belong unambiguously to their branches, clear of the diamond, elbow, stroke, and arrowhead.
- Paths have space on both sides. Follow every horizontal/vertical segment through the full figure: it clears all unrelated text, nodes, markers, headings, and arrowheads.
- Arrow crossings and shared trunks express the intended topology. An accidental crossing or merged branch needs another reserved lane.
- Feedback rails stay in their reserved lanes. rotated labels sit beside them. The feedback destination and direction match the process.

## Whole-figure pass

- Commands and facts match the source. Arrows describe actual flow. decoration does not invent an extra step or feedback loop.
- Columns align. gaps feel consistent. sections provide enough room for their contents. Excess empty space can be reduced without squeezing labels or elbows.
- The canvas contains all content, the footer, and outer rails. No clipped text, disconnected tips, or unexplained connectors remain.
